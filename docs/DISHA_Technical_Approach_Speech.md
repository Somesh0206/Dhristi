### 🎙️ 1-Minute Spoken Script (Complete Pitch)

"Distinguished judges, welcome to **DISHA** — our proactive Geo-Intelligence platform engineered to transition disaster response from reactive chaos to deterministic, life-saving automation.

Technically, DISHA is built entirely on a **Full-Stack JavaScript and Node.js framework** utilizing **React JSX**, **Next.js 14 App Router**, and **HTML5 with Tailwind CSS**.

Our platform runs on a **4-tier modular architecture**:

First, the **Telemetry Ingestion Layer** processes live IoT rainfall, pore-water pressure, and soil saturation to calculate slope stability factors.

Second, the **Geospatial Engine** merges Leaflet and GeoJSON with OpenStreetMap OSRM road networks to compute hazard-free evacuation corridors while balancing population load against safe shelter structural capacities.

Third, our **Autonomous Dispatch Engine** prioritizes citizen SOS beacons and mobilizes rescue squads.

Finally, the **Client Operations Tier** delivers offline continuity, bilingual AI Voice assistance via *Vaani*, AES-256 encrypted responder chat, and role-gated official PDF audits.

By fusing real-time spatial computation with a unified JavaScript architecture, DISHA delivers instant, mathematically grounded disaster response."
