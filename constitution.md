# Dhristi AI Platform Constitution

## Preamble
The Dhristi Disaster Intelligence platform operates to safeguard lives, infrastructure, and vulnerable communities from catastrophic natural hazards through real-time geospatial analytics, AI-assisted early warnings, and coordinated evacuation routing.

## Article I: Safety and Integrity of Life
- All spatial hazard calculations must report accurate geotechnical risks.
- Evacuation directions must never route users through compromised bridges or active landslide ravines.

## Article II: Operational Transparency
- The State Emergency Operations Console (SEOC) must maintain complete visibility into citizen distress beacons, rescue squad assignments, and real-time vehicle vectors.

## Article III: Universal Access & Language Equity
- Information must be readily available in both English and Hindi, accessible via standard web interaction or natural voice interaction through the Dhristi Vaani AI Voice Assistant.
